LD-UI-01: Khong the them bac gio dau tien khi sua chinh sach sang thoi vu
Muc do: Loi giao dien da tai hien.
Buoc tai hien:
1. Dang nhap admin, vao Doi tac lao dong > Chinh sach hoa hong.
2. Mo Sua cho "Chinh sach hoa hong mau Lao dong" v1.
3. Bo chon "Hoa hong chinh thuc", chon "Hoa hong thoi vu".
4. Tim dong bac gio va nut "+ Bac", sau do bam "Luu chinh sach".
Ket qua thuc te: Khong co dong bac gio, khong co nut "+ Bac". Luu khong thanh cong vi can it nhat mot bac gio; mo lai van thay thoi vu tat.
Ket qua mong doi: Co the them bac gio dau tien va nhap don gia de luu chinh sach.
Du lieu sau test: Chinh sach mau khong thay doi.

LD-DATA-01: DEMO-LD-B khong tinh duoc ky thang 11
Bon nguon gioi thieu thoi vu cua doi tac dang gan chinh sach mau chi ho tro hoa hong chinh thuc. Thu tinh ky qua form voi 1 gio cho mot lao dong: khong co ky moi. Day la cau hinh du lieu khong tuong thich; bo tinh thoi vu da hoat dong voi doi tac/chinh sach QA hop le.

LD-DATA-02: Khong luu duoc thay doi doi tac DEMO-LD-B bang form hien tai
Email mau cua doi tac la dia chi @example.invalid. Khi thu chon chinh sach thoi vu mac dinh va bam Luu thay doi, form khong luu vi email khong duoc chap nhan. Da tai lai de xac nhan gia tri mac dinh van la "Khong chon". Khong sua email mau de tranh thay doi thong tin lien he. Chua ket luan bo kiem tra email sai.

LD-BIZ-01: Doi soat thoi vu dung gio nhap tay
Lao dong QA cham cong 0h05; ky doi soat nhap 1 gio tinh 100.000 VND theo don gia 100.000 VND/gio. Bam Tinh lai sau khi co gio ra van giu 1 gio va 100.000 VND. Giao dien neu ro dung du lieu nhap tay. Neu yeu cau nghiep vu la tu dong lay gio cham cong thi luong hien tai chua dap ung.

LD-UI-02: Nut Hoan tac van hien tren khoan chi da hoan tac
Sau khi hoan tac khoan chi QA 60.000 VND, nut Hoan tac tren khoan chi goc van hien. Bam lai khong tao giao dich am thu hai, so du khong doi. Du lieu duoc bao ve, nhung nut con hien gay hieu nham.

LD-UI-03: Nut Ghi nhan tiep van hien sau khi da du gio vao va gio ra
Sau khi lao dong mau P02 da co gio vao va gio ra, nut "Ghi nhan tiep" van bam duoc. Bam lai khong doi gio cong va khong tao ban ghi moi. Du lieu duoc bao ve, nhung thao tac tren giao dien khong co ket qua.
